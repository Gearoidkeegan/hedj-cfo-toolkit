# Sample: Aurelia Werke GmbH

Two invented documents for trying the policy review, and for demonstrations.

- `aurelia-werke-policy.docx` — a short foreign exchange policy of the kind a mid-sized company
  often has: a page or two, written years ago, silent on several things a lender or auditor
  would expect. It is deliberately styled unlike the toolkit's own house style (a different
  body font, heading font, heading colour and unnumbered headings), so `--style match` has
  something visibly different to match against.
- `aurelia-werke-facility.md` — an extract from a facility agreement with the company's lender.
  Clause 22.3 requires at least 60 per cent of drawn amounts to be at fixed rates for three
  years. The policy says nothing about interest rate risk, so the two contradict each other.

Every name, figure and relationship here is invented. Aurelia Werke GmbH does not exist, and
neither do its lenders or suppliers.

Try it with:

```
/cfo-toolkit:policy-review
```

and point it at this folder.
