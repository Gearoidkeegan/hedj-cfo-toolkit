# Facility Agreement (extract)

**Borrower:** Aurelia Werke GmbH
**Lender:** Brennwald Landesbank
**Facility:** Term Loan A, EUR 40,000,000, amortising, secured
**Margin:** 1.50 per cent per annum over EURIBOR, subject to the margin ratchet in clause 9.4

*This is an invented extract from a fictional agreement, written for demonstration.*

## 21. Financial covenants

21.1 The Borrower shall ensure that Net Debt to EBITDA does not exceed 3.00:1 at each Test Date.

21.2 The Borrower shall ensure that Interest Cover is not less than 4.00:1 at each Test Date.

21.3 Test Dates are 30 June and 31 December in each year, and the Borrower shall deliver a
compliance certificate within 45 days of each Test Date.

## 22. Hedging

22.1 The Borrower shall maintain hedging arrangements consistent with its treasury policy, as
approved by its board from time to time, and shall provide a copy of that policy on request.

22.2 Hedging transactions shall be entered into only with the Lender or another financial
institution approved by the Lender in writing.

22.3 The Borrower shall ensure that, within 90 days of the first Utilisation Date and for a
period of not less than three years, at least 60 per cent of drawn amounts under Term Loan A
bear interest at a fixed rate, whether by fixed rate loan, interest rate swap or interest rate cap.

22.4 The Borrower shall not enter into any derivative transaction for speculative purposes.

## 23. Information

23.1 The Borrower shall deliver its audited annual financial statements within 120 days of each
financial year end, and unaudited semi-annual statements within 60 days of each half year end.

23.2 The Borrower shall notify the Lender promptly on becoming aware of any breach of its
treasury policy that is material in the context of the facilities.
