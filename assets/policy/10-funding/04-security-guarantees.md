## Short

The finance director maintains a register of every asset pledged as security and every guarantee
given by the company or a controlled entity, and no new security or guarantee is given without
board approval.

## Full

Where the company's borrowings are secured, or a group entity has given a guarantee in support of
another entity's borrowing, the finance director maintains a register recording the asset or
entity concerned, the facility it secures, and the value of the security or the guarantee's
maximum exposure.

No new security is granted over an asset of the company or a controlled entity, and no new
guarantee is given on behalf of another entity, without board approval. This applies equally to a
guarantee given for a group entity's borrowing and to a guarantee or comfort letter given for a
commercial counterparty, such as a lease or a supply contract.

The finance director reviews the register at each review of this policy under the approval and
review cycle clause, and confirms that no security or guarantee has been given, released or
called since the last review without being recorded.
