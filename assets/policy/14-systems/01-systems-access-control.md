## Short

The company controls access to its treasury and banking systems by role, removes access
promptly when a role ends or changes, and reviews who holds access at each review of this policy
under the approval and review cycle clause.

## Full

Every system the company uses to execute, record or report a treasury transaction, including
online banking, a dealing platform and any treasury management system, is accessed only by a
person whose role requires it, with access rights matched to what the delegated authority
limits clause and the segregation of duties clause allow that person to do. A person is not
given a system right beyond what their role and delegated authority need.

Access is granted only on the finance director's authorisation, is reviewed against the current
authority matrix at each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause, and is
removed no later than the person's last working day or the day their role changes to one that no
longer needs it, consistent with the mandates and signatories clause. Where a system offers
multi-factor authentication, the company uses it for every treasury system.

A change to which systems the company uses for treasury, or to how access to them is
controlled, is reported to the board, and the record of who holds access to each system is kept
under the records and audit trail clause.
