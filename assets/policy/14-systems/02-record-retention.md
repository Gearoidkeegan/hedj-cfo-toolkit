## Short

The company retains trade and confirmation records for at least {{sys-retention-years}}, kept
accessible to its auditors.

## Full

The company retains a record of every trade confirmation, hedge designation, counterparty
agreement and payment instruction for at least {{sys-retention-years}} from the date of the
transaction or, where later, from the date the record was last relied on, whichever gives the
longer period. Where a longer period is required by law, by a facility agreement or by the
company's own document retention policy, the longer period applies instead.

Records are kept in a form that remains readable for the whole retention period, and accessible
to the finance director, the board and the company's auditors on request. A record is not
destroyed before its retention period ends even where the transaction or relationship it
relates to has itself ended.
