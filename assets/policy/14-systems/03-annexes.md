## Short

This policy opens with a table of the board's decisions; authority limits are recorded in the
authority matrix.

## Full

The board's decisions under this policy, including every limit, ratio and threshold left open in
the clauses above, are set out as a table at the front of this policy, showing the current,
board-approved figure for each.

The delegated authority limits set out under the delegated authority limits clause are recorded
separately, in an authority matrix approved by the board alongside this policy. The finance
director keeps the authority matrix up to date as roles and limits change, and reconciles it to
the table of board decisions at each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle
clause.
