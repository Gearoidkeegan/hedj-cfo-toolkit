## Short

Surplus cash is invested in this order of priority: preservation of capital, then liquidity, then
yield. Yield never comes first.

## Full

The company invests surplus cash it does not need for its immediate operating and liquidity plan
in the following order of priority: first, preservation of capital, so that the amount invested
is returned in full at maturity; second, liquidity, so that the investment can be realised when
the company needs the cash; and third, yield.

No investment is made for yield alone where doing so would compromise the preservation of capital
or the liquidity the company's forecast requires. Surplus cash not committed to investment under
this order of priority is held on deposit under the permitted instruments clause.

This order of priority governs every investment decision the company makes with surplus cash,
regardless of the return available on an instrument that would rank capital or liquidity behind
yield.
