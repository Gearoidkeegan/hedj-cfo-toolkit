## Short

Where a facility or hedge references an interest rate benchmark that is discontinued or
materially changed, the company follows the fallback set out in the facility or instrument, and
confirms with its counterparty that any replacement remains commercially equivalent.

## Full

The company's floating rate borrowings and any interest rate hedge against them reference a
published interest rate benchmark. Where the administrator of a benchmark discontinues it,
materially changes how it is calculated, or the regulator responsible for it announces that it
is no longer representative, the company follows the fallback provisions set out in the facility
agreement or the hedging documentation, rather than negotiating a replacement rate unilaterally.

Before a fallback takes effect, the finance director confirms with the lender or hedging
counterparty that the replacement rate, and any adjustment applied alongside it, keeps the
economic terms of the borrowing or hedge broadly unchanged. Where a facility or hedge is silent
on what happens if its benchmark is discontinued, the finance director raises this with the
counterparty and reports the position to the board under the reporting to the board clause,
before the benchmark is due to be discontinued.
