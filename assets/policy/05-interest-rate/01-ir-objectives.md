## Short

The company manages the risk that interest rate movements affect the cost of its borrowings,
balancing the certainty of a fixed rate against the flexibility of a floating one.

## Full

Interest rate risk is the risk that a movement in interest rates increases the cost of the
company's borrowings compared with what was planned when the borrowing was arranged. The
company's objective is to keep the cost of its debt within a range it can plan around, not to
profit from a view on where rates will move.

The company manages this risk through the mix of fixed and floating rate debt set out in the
fixed and floating mix clause, and, where the board permits, through the instruments set out in
the permitted instruments clause of this section.
