## Short

Where a facility agreement requires a minimum proportion of debt to be hedged or a minimum term
of hedging, the company meets that requirement in addition to, not instead of, the fixed and
floating mix set out in this section.

## Full

A facility agreement may itself require the company to hedge a minimum proportion of the debt
drawn under it, to a minimum term, as a condition of the facility. Where it does, that
requirement is a term of the facility, not of this policy, and the company complies with it for
as long as the facility is outstanding, in addition to the fixed and floating mix clause of this
policy.

Where a facility's own requirement is higher than this policy's, the facility's requirement
governs the drawn debt to which it applies. The finance director records every facility-level
hedging requirement and reports compliance with it to the board under the reporting to the board
clause.
