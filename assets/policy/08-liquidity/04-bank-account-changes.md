## Short

Opening, closing or changing signatories on a bank account requires finance director approval and
two director signatures; the finance team updates its account register.

## Full

No bank account may be opened, closed, or have its signatories or online banking access changed,
without the approval of the finance director and the signature of two directors. This applies
equally to an account opened for a new entity, a new currency or a specific project.

The finance director maintains a register of every account the company and its controlled
entities hold, recording the bank, account number, currency, signatories and the purpose of the
account. The register is updated within five business days of any change, and reconciled to the
company's own records at each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause.

An account dormant for twelve months is reviewed for closure, unless the finance director records
a business reason to keep it open.
