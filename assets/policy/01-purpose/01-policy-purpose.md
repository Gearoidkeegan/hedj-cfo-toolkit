## Short

This policy sets out how the company manages its treasury risks, who may act,
and within what limits. Its purpose is to protect the company's cash flow and
earnings from financial market movements, not to profit from them.

## Full

This policy sets out how the company identifies, measures, manages and reports
its treasury risks: foreign exchange, interest rate, commodity, counterparty
and liquidity. It states who may act on the company's behalf, within what
limits, and how those actions are reported to the board.

The company's treasury objectives are, in order of priority: to ensure the
company can meet its obligations as they fall due; to protect cash flow and
earnings from movements in financial markets; and to manage the cost of
funding. The company does not take positions in financial markets for profit,
and no transaction may be entered into that is not matched to an underlying
business exposure.

## Full (hedging)

This policy sets out how the company identifies, measures, manages and
reports the risks arising from its hedging activity: foreign exchange,
interest rate and commodity risk, and the counterparty risk that hedging
them creates. It states who may act on the company's behalf, within what
limits, and how those actions are reported to the board.

The company's hedging objective is to protect cash flow and earnings from
movements in financial markets. No transaction may be entered into that is
not matched to an underlying business exposure, and the company does not
take positions in financial markets for profit.
