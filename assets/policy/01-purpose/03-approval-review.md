## Short

The directors approve this policy and review it {{review-frequency}}, or sooner if the company's
exposures or structure change materially. Between reviews, only the directors may amend it.

## Full

The directors approve this policy before it takes effect, and every subsequent version. The
directors review the policy {{review-frequency}}, and also whenever the company's currencies,
borrowings, structure or exposures change in a way that could make the policy out of date.

A review checks whether the objectives, limits and roles set out in this policy still fit the
company's activities, and whether any exception granted since the last review should become a
permanent change. The finance director prepares the review for the directors; only the directors
may approve a change to the policy.
