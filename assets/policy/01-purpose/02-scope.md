## Short

This policy applies to the company and every entity it controls, and to every currency,
interest rate, commodity and cash exposure arising from their business. It does not cover
trading, speculation, or any transaction entered into for a purpose other than managing
that exposure.

## Full

This policy applies to the company and to every entity that it controls, wherever they are
incorporated or operate, and it covers every treasury exposure arising from their business
activities: foreign exchange, interest rate, commodity, counterparty and liquidity. It applies
to every currency in which the company or a controlled entity transacts, and to every bank
account, facility and instrument used to manage those exposures.

This policy does not apply to a person's own financial affairs, or to any transaction entered
into other than in the ordinary course of the company's business. It does not permit trading
or speculative activity: every transaction must be matched to an underlying exposure that the
company or a controlled entity actually has.

## Short (hedging)

This policy applies to the company and every entity it controls, and to every currency,
interest rate and commodity exposure arising from their business. It does not cover
trading, speculation, or any transaction entered into for a purpose other than managing
that exposure.

## Full (hedging)

This policy applies to the company and to every entity that it controls, wherever they are
incorporated or operate, and it covers every hedging exposure arising from their business
activities: foreign exchange, interest rate, commodity and counterparty. It applies to every
currency in which the company or a controlled entity transacts, and to every bank account,
facility and instrument used to manage those exposures.

This policy does not apply to a person's own financial affairs, or to any transaction entered
into other than in the ordinary course of the company's business. It does not permit trading
or speculative activity: every transaction must be matched to an underlying exposure that the
company or a controlled entity actually has.
