## Short

Any departure from this policy requires the board's approval before it is acted on, except in
an emergency, when it is reported to the board as soon as possible afterwards. Every exception
is recorded, with its reason and its duration.

## Full

This policy applies unless the board approves an exception. An exception is sought and approved
before the transaction or activity it relates to takes place, is limited to a stated transaction
or duration, and does not by itself change the policy for the future.

In an emergency, where seeking approval in advance is not practical, the finance director may
act outside the policy to protect the company's position, and reports the action to the board as
soon as possible afterwards, together with the reason it could not wait for approval.

Every exception, whether approved in advance or reported afterwards, is recorded in the register
of exceptions, with the date, the reason, the person who approved it and, where relevant, the
date it expires.
