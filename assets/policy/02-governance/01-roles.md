## Short

This policy sets out who may act for the company in treasury matters: the board, which sets
policy and limits; the finance director, who manages treasury day to day; and the individuals
who execute and approve each transaction. A person who executes a transaction does not also
approve it.

## Full

The board sets this policy, sets the limits within which others may act, approves any exception,
and receives regular reporting on treasury activity. It does not itself execute transactions.

The finance director, or the person who carries out that function, is responsible for treasury
on a day-to-day basis: identifying and measuring exposures, arranging hedges and facilities
within the limits the board has set, keeping records, and escalating anything outside those
limits.

Every treasury transaction has a person who executes it and a person who approves it; under the
segregation of duties clause, these are not the same person. Where the finance team is too small
to separate the two roles between different individuals for every transaction, a director who
took no part in the transaction reviews it instead, and that review is itself recorded.
