## Short

The board sets the limits within which each role may act, and no transaction is entered into
outside them. Hedging transactions are limited to {{limit-trade-execute}}.

## Full

The board delegates authority to act within the limits set out below, and reviews them as part
of each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause. No person may enter
into a transaction outside the limit that applies to their role, and no limit may be exceeded by
splitting a transaction.

Hedging transactions may be executed up to {{limit-trade-execute}}. A transaction above this
limit requires approval by the board or by two directors. This clause sets the limit on hedging
transactions only; where this policy also covers payments and transfers between the company's
own accounts, a separate limit applies to those.

Limits are recorded in the authority matrix the board approves with this policy, together with
the person holding each delegation. A change of role does not carry a limit with it: the matrix is updated
before the new holder acts.
