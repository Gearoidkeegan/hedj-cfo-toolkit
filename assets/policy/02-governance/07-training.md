## Short

Everyone who holds a delegated authority under this policy is trained in that authority, the
instruments they may use and the limits that apply, before they use it, and again whenever the
policy, their role or the instruments used change. Training completed is recorded for each
person.

## Full

Before a person exercises a delegated authority under this policy, they are trained in this
policy, the transactions and instruments relevant to their role, the limits that apply to them,
and how to recognise and escalate a breach under the breaches and escalation clause.

Training is refreshed whenever this policy changes in a way that affects the person's role,
whenever the person's role or delegated authority changes, and whenever a new instrument or
facility is introduced that the person will use. Where the finance team relies on an outside
adviser for specialist input, the finance director remains responsible for understanding enough
of the subject to exercise their own authority under this policy, rather than leaving that
authority to the adviser.

A record of training completed, by person and by date, is kept under the records and audit trail
clause.
