## Short

A breach is any transaction, payment or exposure that falls outside a limit set by the board or
outside a person's delegated authority. A breach is escalated to the finance director
immediately and to the board without delay, and is recorded together with the corrective action
taken.

## Full

A breach of this policy occurs when a transaction is entered into, a payment is made, or an
exposure arises, outside the limits set under the delegated authority limits clause, or
otherwise contrary to a rule in this policy. Splitting a transaction to stay within a limit is
itself a breach.

Whoever identifies a breach reports it to the finance director immediately. The finance director
assesses whether the breach needs to be unwound or otherwise remedied, takes the action needed
to protect the company's position, and reports the breach to the board without delay, whatever
its size.

Every breach is recorded in the register of exceptions, together with the corrective action
taken and any change made to prevent it recurring. A pattern of breaches in the same area is
treated as a sign that the policy, the limits or the roles need to be reviewed, not only that the
individual breach needs to be corrected.
