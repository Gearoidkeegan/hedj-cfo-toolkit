## Short

The board receives a treasury report {{reporting-frequency}}, covering the company's exposures,
the hedges and facilities in place, compliance with the limits in this policy, and any breach or
exception. A report is also given after any breach, without waiting for the next scheduled
report.

## Full

The finance director reports to the board on treasury matters {{reporting-frequency}}. Each
report covers: the company's material exposures by currency, interest rate basis and, where
relevant, commodity; the hedges, facilities and cash balances held against those exposures;
compliance with the limits set out in the delegated authority limits clause and elsewhere in
this policy; and any breach or exception since the last report, with the action taken.

Where a breach or a significant change in exposure arises between scheduled reports, the finance
director reports it to the board without waiting for the next scheduled report, under the
breaches and escalation clause.

The board's minutes record that the report was received and considered.
