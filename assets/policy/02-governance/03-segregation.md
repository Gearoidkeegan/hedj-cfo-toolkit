## Short

The person who executes a treasury transaction does not also approve it, initiate the payment
for it, or reconcile it. Where the finance team is too small to separate every step between
different people, a director who took no part in the transaction reviews it.

## Full

No individual performs more than one of the following steps for the same transaction: proposing
or executing it, approving it under the delegated authority limits clause, initiating the
payment that settles it, and reconciling it to the bank statement or facility record.

Where the size of the finance team makes full separation impractical, the company uses a
compensating control instead: a director who took no part in the transaction reviews it, and the
record of it, before or as soon as possible after it is completed. That review is itself
recorded.

Access to online banking, dealing platforms and payment systems is granted individually and
matches the role each person actually performs; access is removed promptly when a role changes
or ends.
