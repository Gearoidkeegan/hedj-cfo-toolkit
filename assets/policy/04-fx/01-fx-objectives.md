## Short

The company manages the risk that exchange rate movements affect its cash flow and earnings,
hedging to protect that value rather than to profit from it.

## Full

Foreign exchange risk is the risk that a movement in exchange rates changes the value, in the
company's reporting currency, of its foreign currency receipts, payments, assets or liabilities.
The company's objective is to reduce the uncertainty these movements create for its budgeted
cash flow and reported earnings, not to generate profit from a currency position.

The company identifies its foreign currency exposures under the how exposures are measured
clause, and manages them within the hedge ratio bands, permitted instruments and prohibitions
set out in the rest of this section. No foreign exchange transaction is entered into that is not
matched to an underlying exposure of the company or a controlled entity.
