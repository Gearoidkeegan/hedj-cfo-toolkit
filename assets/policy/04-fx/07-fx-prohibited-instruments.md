## Short

The company does not speculate, write (sell) options, or use leveraged, knock-in or knock-out
structures: every transaction is matched to an underlying exposure, not a market view.

## Full

The company does not use foreign exchange instruments to take a speculative position: no
transaction is entered into that is not matched to an underlying exposure of the company or a
controlled entity, and no transaction is entered into in the expectation of profiting from a
view on where a currency will move.

The company does not write (sell) options; every option used under the permitted instruments
clause is purchased. The company does not use a leveraged structure, where a movement in the
exchange rate produces a gain or loss disproportionate to the underlying exposure, and does not
use a knock-in or knock-out structure, where the instrument's protection can disappear, or a new
obligation can arise, on a market trigger unrelated to the size of the exposure being hedged.
