## Short

Transaction exposure arises from the company's foreign currency receipts and payments, forecast
by currency and hedged within the hedge ratio bands set out in this section.

## Full

Transaction exposure arises whenever the company or a controlled entity commits to receive or
pay an amount in a currency other than its reporting currency: a sale, a purchase, a loan, or a
dividend. The value of that receipt or payment, once translated into the reporting currency,
changes as the exchange rate moves between the date the commitment arises and the date it
settles.

The company identifies transaction exposure as soon as it becomes firm or highly probable, on
the basis set out in the forecasting and materiality clause, and hedges it within the hedge
ratio bands by tenor set out in this section. An exposure that is only possible, rather than
firm or highly probable, is monitored but not hedged.
