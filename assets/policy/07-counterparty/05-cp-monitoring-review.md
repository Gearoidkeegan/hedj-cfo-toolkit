## Short

The finance director reviews the approved counterparty list, each counterparty's rating where
one applies, and the company's exposure to each, {{cp-review-frequency}}, and reports the
outcome to the board.

## Full

The finance director reviews the approved counterparty list {{cp-review-frequency}}, checking
that each counterparty is still suitable, that its credit rating, where the board requires a
minimum rating, still meets it, and that the company's exposure to it remains within the
exposure limits and concentration clause. The review also checks whether a
counterparty the company no longer deals with should be removed from the list.

Outside the scheduled review, the finance director monitors public information about an approved
counterparty, such as a rating change or other material news, and brings forward the review of
that counterparty if the information suggests its suitability may have changed. The outcome of
each review, whether scheduled or brought forward, is reported to the board under the reporting
to the board clause.
