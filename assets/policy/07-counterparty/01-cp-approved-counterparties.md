## Short

The company deals only with counterparties the board has approved, under a reviewed agreement.
The finance director keeps the approved list current and reports changes to the board.

## Full

The company enters into a treasury transaction, holds cash, or draws a facility only with a
counterparty the board has approved in advance, on the basis of documentation the finance
director has reviewed. A counterparty is added to the approved list by board decision, not by
the finance director alone, however routine the relationship appears.

The finance director keeps the list of approved counterparties current, records the date each
counterparty was approved and the documentation relied on, and reports any addition or removal
to the board under the reporting to the board clause. A new instrument or product with an
existing approved counterparty does not itself require re-approval of the counterparty, but,
where this policy addresses that category of risk, is checked against the instruments it
permits for it.
