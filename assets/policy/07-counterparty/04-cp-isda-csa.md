## Short

Every derivative the company enters into with a counterparty is documented under a signed ISDA
Master Agreement and, where the board requires collateral to be posted or received, a Credit
Support Annex. The finance director keeps a signed copy of each on file.

## Full

Before the company enters into a derivative with a counterparty for the first time, the finance
director puts in place a signed ISDA Master Agreement with that counterparty, covering every
derivative transacted under it, together with a schedule that records the elections the company
and the counterparty have agreed, such as the events that allow a party to close out the
agreement early.

Where the company's exposure to a counterparty, or the counterparty's exposure to the company,
under open derivatives could reach a level the board considers material, the finance director
puts in place a Credit Support Annex that sets out when collateral is posted or received, in
what form, and how it is valued. The company does not enter into a derivative with a
counterparty before that counterparty's ISDA Master Agreement, and any Credit Support Annex the
board has required, is signed.

The finance director keeps a signed copy of each ISDA Master Agreement, its schedule and any
Credit Support Annex on file, together with a summary of the key commercial terms that is
reviewed whenever the agreement is amended.
