## Short

This section covers price risk from a commodity or energy input the company buys, or sells,
where movements have a material effect on cost of sales or revenue.

## Full

Commodity risk is the risk that a movement in the price of a raw material, an energy input, or a
commodity the company sells affects its cost of sales, its margin or its revenue. This section
applies to each commodity the company has identified as a material exposure, whether because the
company buys it as an input or sells it as part of its output.

A commodity is a material exposure where a plausible price movement would have a significant
effect on the company's cost of sales or revenue, judged on the same basis as the forecasting
and materiality clause applies to other exposures. A commodity the company uses only in small or
occasional quantities is monitored but not separately identified as a risk under this section.
