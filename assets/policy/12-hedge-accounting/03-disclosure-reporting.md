## Short

Where the company applies hedge accounting, it discloses the nature of its hedging activity and
the result of effectiveness testing in its financial statements, as its reporting framework
requires, and reports the position to the board under the reporting to the board clause.

## Full

Where the company applies hedge accounting to a hedge relationship, it discloses the nature and
extent of its hedging activity, the risk it hedges, and the outcome of the effectiveness testing
carried out under the effectiveness testing clause, in its financial statements to the extent its
reporting framework requires. Where the company does not apply hedge accounting to a hedge it
holds, the hedging instrument is accounted for, and disclosed, on whatever other basis its
reporting framework prescribes.

Alongside the financial statement disclosure, the finance director reports the company's hedge
accounting position to the board under the reporting to the board clause: which hedge
relationships are designated, whether each remains effective, and any relationship for which
hedge accounting has been discontinued since the last report.

A change in the company's reporting framework, or in how that framework treats hedge accounting,
is itself reported to the board, together with its effect on hedges already designated.
