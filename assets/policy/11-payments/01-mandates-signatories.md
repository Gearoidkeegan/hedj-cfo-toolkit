## Short

Bank mandates name only current directors or employees the board authorises, within their
delegated authority, and are updated whenever a signatory's role changes.

## Full

Every bank mandate names only current directors or employees the board has specifically
authorised as signatories, and states the authority each one holds, consistent with the delegated
authority limits clause. No person is added to a mandate other than with the finance director's
approval and the signature of two directors, consistent with the bank account opening and closing
clause.

A mandate is updated to remove a signatory within five business days of that person leaving the
company or changing role in a way that ends their authority to sign, and the finance director
confirms, at each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause, that every
mandate matches the current authority matrix.

Online banking access is granted and removed on the same basis as signing authority, and a
person's access is removed no later than their last working day.
