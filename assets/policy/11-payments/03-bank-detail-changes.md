## Short

A change to a supplier's bank details is verified by phone to a known, independently sourced
number, before any payment reaches the new account.

## Full

A request to change a supplier's, employee's or other payee's bank account details, however it is
received, including by email that appears to come from the payee, is not acted on until it is
verified by a phone call to a number the company already holds on file or sources independently,
never a number given in the request itself.

The person who receives the request is not the person who verifies it and updates the payment
record, consistent with the segregation of duties clause. No payment is made to the new account
until the verification is complete and recorded, and the previous account details are kept on
file in case the change needs to be reversed.

A request that cannot be verified, or that shows any sign of being fraudulent, is refused and
reported to the finance director immediately.
