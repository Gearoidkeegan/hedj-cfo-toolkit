## Short

Payment instructions received by email or phone are never acted on without independent
verification; every employee handling payments is trained to recognise and report fraud.

## Full

An instruction to make a payment, change a payment, or redirect a payment, received by email,
phone or any channel other than the company's own systems, is treated as unverified until it is
confirmed independently, including an instruction that appears to come from a director or senior
manager. Urgency, seniority or confidentiality in the request is treated as a warning sign, not a
reason to bypass verification.

Every employee who initiates, approves or releases payments is trained, when they start and
again at least as often as each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle
clause, to recognise a suspicious payment request and the company's process for verifying and
reporting one. A suspected fraud is reported to the finance director
immediately, whether or not a payment has been made, and, where a payment has been made, the
company's bank and the police are notified without delay.

Controls under this clause apply in addition to, not instead of, the supplier bank detail changes
clause and the dual authorisation and limits clause.
