## Short

The company confirms how each counterparty that provides it with a financial instrument or
investment service categorises it under MiFID, reviews that categorisation when the relationship
begins, whenever a counterparty notifies a change, and at each review of this policy under the
approval and review cycle clause, and records the basis for it.

## Full

Each counterparty the company deals with for a hedging transaction or an investment of surplus
cash categorises the company as a client under MiFID, in its EU form or its UK equivalent
depending on where the counterparty is established, and that categorisation determines the
protections and information the counterparty must give the company. This policy does not assume
which category applies: the company's size, activity and any election it makes with a
counterparty all affect the outcome.

The finance director confirms, for each counterparty providing a relevant service, how that
counterparty currently categorises the company, and keeps the confirmation on file alongside the
approved counterparties list maintained under the approved counterparties clause. Where the
company could ask a counterparty to treat it with more protection, or with less, that election is
a board decision, taken only after the company understands what the change would gain or give up.

The categorisation held with each counterparty is reviewed when the relationship with that
counterparty begins, whenever the counterparty notifies the company of a change, and as part of
each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause, and the outcome is
reported to the board.
