## Short

This policy identifies and manages risk arising from movements in foreign exchange rates,
interest rates and commodity prices, together with counterparty risk and liquidity risk, each
covered in its own section of this policy to the extent it arises from the company's activities.

## Full

This policy identifies, measures and manages five categories of treasury risk, each addressed in
its own section: foreign exchange risk, arising from receipts, payments, assets or liabilities in
a currency other than the company's reporting currency; interest rate risk, arising from
borrowing or investing at a rate that can change; commodity risk, arising from the price of an
input or output the company buys or sells; counterparty risk, arising from the possibility that a
bank or other party the company deals with fails to perform; and liquidity risk, the risk that
the company cannot meet an obligation when it falls due.

A category that does not currently arise from the company's activities is not actively managed,
but this policy continues to cover it if it arises in future.

## Short (hedging)

This policy identifies and manages risk arising from movements in foreign exchange rates,
interest rates and commodity prices, together with the counterparty risk that hedging them
creates, each covered in its own section of this policy to the extent it arises from the
company's activities.

## Full (hedging)

This policy identifies, measures and manages four categories of risk arising from the
company's hedging activity, each addressed in its own section: foreign exchange risk, arising
from receipts, payments, assets or liabilities in a currency other than the company's reporting
currency; interest rate risk, arising from borrowing or investing at a rate that can change;
commodity risk, arising from the price of an input or output the company buys or sells; and
counterparty risk, arising from the possibility that a bank or other party the company deals
with fails to perform.

A category that does not currently arise from the company's activities is not actively managed,
but this policy continues to cover it if it arises in future.
