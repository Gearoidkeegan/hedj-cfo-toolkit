## Short

The board's appetite for treasury risk is expressed through the limits, hedge ratio bands and
thresholds set out elsewhere in this policy, not as a single risk appetite figure. The company
manages risk to protect its cash flow and earnings, and does not take positions in financial
markets for profit.

## Full

The board's appetite for treasury risk is expressed through the limits, hedge ratio bands,
permitted instruments and thresholds set out in the sections of this policy that address each
category of risk, rather than as a single risk appetite number. Taken together, those limits
describe how much unhedged exposure the board is willing for the company to carry, and in what
circumstances.

The company's risk appetite favours protecting its cash flow and earnings over the possibility
of a better outcome from an unhedged position: the minimum hedge ratios and fixed-rate
proportions set elsewhere in this policy default to certainty. Within the limits those minimums
leave, the board may choose an instrument, or retain a balance unhedged, that keeps some benefit
from a favourable market move; that choice does not itself change the company's appetite for the
exposure it still leaves uncovered. The company does not take a position in a financial
instrument that is not matched to an underlying business exposure, and does not use treasury
activity as a source of profit.

The board revisits whether the limits set under this policy still reflect its risk appetite at
each review of this policy under the approval and review cycle clause, and sooner if the
company's circumstances change materially.
