## Short

The company forecasts its material exposures over a horizon long enough to plan and execute a
hedge before the exposure crystallises, and updates the forecast regularly. An exposure that
could have a significant effect on cash flow or earnings is treated as material; smaller
exposures are monitored but not actively hedged.

## Full

The company prepares a rolling forecast of its exposures in each currency, interest rate basis
and, where relevant, commodity, covering a horizon long enough to allow a hedge to be arranged
before the exposure needs to be covered. The forecast is updated regularly, and more often where
the underlying business is volatile or forecasting has proved unreliable.

An exposure is treated as material if, left unhedged, it could have a significant effect on the
company's cash flow or reported earnings; the finance director judges materiality against the
company's overall scale rather than against a fixed threshold. A material exposure is forecast
and actively managed under the relevant section of this policy; an exposure that is not material
is monitored but not actively hedged, and is escalated if it grows to become material.
