Three reviewers have read a treasury policy review for {{company_name}}: a
lender, an auditor and a non-executive director. Some findings they endorsed,
some they disputed, and some they added.

Rule on each finding that is disputed or added. For each, return one verdict:
- `keep` — the finding stands.
- `drop` — the finding is wrong, duplicated, or so minor it wastes the
  reader's attention.
- `disagreement` — the reviewers genuinely disagree and a CFO should see both
  sides. Say both sides in your reason.

Judge severity honestly: a policy that is merely shorter than a large
company's is not defective. Do not add findings of your own, and do not soften
a finding to avoid disagreement. A contradiction proved against a facility
document is not yours to drop.
